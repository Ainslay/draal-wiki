#przedmioty 

Przekute przez [[Postaci/Bohaterowie/Thorgrim\|Thorgrima]] w [[Lokacje/Dolna Pustynia/Klanaryt/Pierwsze Miasto/Kuźnia Woli\|Kuźni Woli]] odłamki [[Przedmioty/Magiczne/Młot Istari\|Młota Istari]] oraz [[Przedmioty/Magiczne/Topór Uwiądu\|Topór Uwiądu]] utworzyły nową broń łączącą w unię potęgę obu artefaktów w postać dwuręcznego topora.

Topór posiada zdolności związane ze światłem [[Religia/Zodiak/Kemel Aratan\|Kemel Aratan]] i jest opatrzony Runą Powrotu Istari, zapewniającą że przedmiot wróci do [[Frakcje/Istari, Plemię Gromu\|Rodzimej wioski właściciela]] po jego śmierci.

## Vaelrath
Dostrojenie 2
#### Festering Aura
If you use Tempest Discharge (3 charges), until the end of your next turn your Aura of Protection reduces the next saving throw made by an enemy.
#### Rotting Deceit
When you hit a creature that is under the effect of an illusion, invisibility, shape-change, or a magical disguise for the first time, you no longer have disadvantage on subsequent attack rolls against that creature. 

Additionally, the creature must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, at the start of each of its turns while it maintains the effect, it takes an additional 1d8 necrotic damage.
#### Rot Exposes Lies
A Humanoid that comes into contact with the axe after it has been activated must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, each time the creature tells a lie, it takes 3d8 necrotic damage. This damage is cumulative.

You can use this feature once a day (not long rest).
#### Defiance
If your hit points are reduced to 0, you can choose to continue fighting until the end of your next turn. Doing so causes you to automatically fail one death saving throw.

At the end of that turn, you can choose to automatically fail a second death saving throw and remain conscious until the end of your following turn. Otherwise, you fall unconscious as normal.

At the end of the third turn fought in this way, you die.

You can use this feature once before finishing a long rest.
